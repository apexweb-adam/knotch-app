Knotch support

Counting on Apple Watch

Tap anywhere on the counter or turn the Digital Crown forward to finish a row. Turn the crown back to undo. On Apple Watch Series 9, Ultra 2 and later you can also double tap your index finger and thumb. Swipe left for the stitch counter and project actions.

What the buzzes mean

A short click is a row. A rising tap means a repeat is done. A longer buzz means a new section starts. A success pattern means the project is finished.

Importing a pattern

With Knotch Pro, tap + and choose Paste pattern text, Open a PDF or text file, or Read a photo of the page. Knotch turns lines such as "Row 3: k2, p2 to end" or "Rnd 5: sc around" into rows, and "Repeat rows 3 to 4 five more times" into a repeating section. Check the repeats before you start; you can edit them at any time.

The Watch shows an old row

Open Knotch on your iPhone once with the Watch nearby. Both devices keep the newest count.

Subscriptions

Knotch Pro renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel in the iPhone Settings app, under your name, then Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal. To restore a purchase, open Settings in Knotch and tap Restore purchases.
