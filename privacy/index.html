Knotch privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Knotch is a row and stitch counter for crochet and knitting on iPhone and Apple Watch. It is operated by Tokár Ádám, sole proprietor, Hungary.

The short version

Knotch itself collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and Knotch does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Free users can choose to watch one optional ad video, covered in the Ads section below. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your devices

  • Your projects, patterns, counts and notes are stored on your iPhone and your Apple Watch. The two devices sync directly with each other through Apple's Watch connection. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • When you import a PDF or text file, Knotch reads the text on your iPhone. The file is not uploaded.
  • When you read a photo of a pattern page, the text is recognised on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. Knotch can only see the photo you pick; it cannot read the rest of your photo library.
  • The trial reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn it off on the paywall or in iOS Settings.

Ads

Knotch shows an optional rewarded video to free users who want one more project. The video comes from Google AdMob. Ads are not personalised. Google may collect your device identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address), ad interaction and diagnostic data to deliver and measure the ad. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are first asked for consent, and you can change it any time in Settings under Ad privacy choices. Knotch Pro removes all ads.

Purchases

Knotch Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

Children

Knotch itself does not collect data from anyone, including children. The optional ad video is covered in the Ads section above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
